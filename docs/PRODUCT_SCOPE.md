# AI Friendship Product Scope

AI Friendship is an AI companion for supportive everyday conversation and creative work.

Core scope:
- transparent AI companion identity
- general + creative chat
- user-controlled companion preferences
- conversation history
- user-approved memory
- privacy/data controls
- account/cloud foundations
- safety/dependency boundaries
- responsive accessible web experience

Out of scope as current verified functionality: clinical care, emergency service, fake human relationship, unverified paid entitlement, or claims of guaranteed safety/privacy.
