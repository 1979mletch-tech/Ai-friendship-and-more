# Final Human Review Checklist

Review the actual deployed candidate for:
- product identity and no fake-human claims
- no dead navigation/buttons
- account/auth clarity
- chat quality and fallback transparency
- user-controlled memory/history
- privacy/export/delete clarity
- safety/dependency wording
- mobile usability
- accessibility basics
- pricing labels and billing truthfulness
- error states
- no placeholder/demo copy presented as production functionality
- legal/privacy/support links appropriate to launch

Human review complements automated tests; it does not replace them.
