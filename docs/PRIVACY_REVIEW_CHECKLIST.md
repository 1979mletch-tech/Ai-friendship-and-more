# Privacy Review Checklist

Before production:
- verify conversation/memory content never enters analytics events;
- verify no health/financial/private conversation content enters URLs;
- verify browser/server error logs exclude message bodies and credentials;
- document AI-provider processing, retention and training settings;
- document database retention and backup behavior;
- verify local export/delete;
- verify cloud account deletion and cascade;
- verify password/session/token handling;
- verify data subject/contact process appropriate to deployment jurisdiction;
- review privacy notice against actual configured providers.

This checklist is not legal certification.
