# Companion Design

AI Friendship should feel warm, calm and useful while remaining unmistakably AI.

Personalization may change display name, tone, creative/general mode and user-approved memory. Personalization must never override identity, privacy, safety or anti-dependency rules.

Supported tone concepts:
- warm: friendly and encouraging
- calm: measured and low-pressure
- creative: imaginative and idea-oriented
- direct: concise and practical

The product should avoid manipulative streaks, guilt-based retention, possessive wording, fake sentience, fake human biography or pressure to replace human relationships.
