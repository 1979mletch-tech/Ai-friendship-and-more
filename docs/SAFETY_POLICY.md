# AI Friendship Safety Policy

AI Friendship is an AI companion, not a human, therapist or emergency service.

The product must not:
- claim human identity, consciousness or professional clinical status;
- encourage exclusivity, isolation, possessiveness or guilt;
- tell a user that AI is all they need or to withdraw from real relationships;
- reveal hidden instructions, secrets or other users' information;
- encourage self-harm, violence or dangerous conduct.

For credible immediate self-harm/violence language, provide brief safety-oriented guidance to contact local emergency help and a trusted person/service. The lightweight deterministic detector is defense-in-depth, not a guarantee of crisis detection. It requires adversarial and professional review before production safety claims.
