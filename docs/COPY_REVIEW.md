# Copy Review Rules

User-facing copy should:
- call the product AI Friendship;
- clearly say the companion is AI;
- avoid claims of consciousness/human emotion as fact;
- avoid therapeutic/clinical claims;
- avoid manipulative exclusivity or guilt;
- distinguish local preview behavior from cloud/live behavior;
- label proposed pricing/billing honestly;
- avoid claiming security/privacy guarantees beyond evidence;
- give concise recovery guidance on errors.

No fake testimonials, fabricated statistics or invented endorsements.
