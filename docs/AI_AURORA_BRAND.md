# AI Aurora Brand

Product name: **AI Aurora**.

Primary line: **Conversation. Creativity. Connection.**

Core promise: a warm AI companion for conversation, creativity and thinking out loud. The brand must remain explicit that Aurora is AI. It must not imply a human relationship, therapy, emergency support, guaranteed confidentiality or emotional exclusivity.

Visual direction: midnight canvas, luminous aurora gradients, soft glass-like surfaces, large editorial typography, generous spacing and a simple A light-mark. The website should feel calm, premium and creative rather than clinical or like a generic chatbot.
